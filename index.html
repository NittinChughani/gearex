06 / THE VISUAL EXPLAINER

Watch variables move. Understand why.

GearEx builds a local execution timeline for foundational statements, loops, functions, arrays and objects. Follow active code lines and watch the call stack, heap and output update. It detects the language as you type.

Runs locally in your browser
Editor ready

The GearEx trace engine is an original, browser-local educational interpreter, not a complete implementation of the seven languages. It accurately models supported fundamental operations, while advanced syntax and libraries may stop with a coverage note. SQL displays a conceptual query plan, not database execution. For actual compilation or complete program results, use the compiler.

LEARN · ASSESS · ACHIEVE

Earn a certificate by demonstrating what you learned.

Each lesson ends with five questions. Pass with 4/5 to complete it. Finish all lessons and pass the final assessment with at least 70%. Your certificate reports lesson-check marks (70%), final assessment marks (30%), and a combined score out of 100.

Certificates are creator-issued records of self-paced completion, not accredited qualifications. A certificate name is confirmed once and then locked in the interface, but browser storage can be modified or cleared; identity and scores are not independently verified.
